Grand Piano

  • It is technically classified as a percussion instrument.
  • It can have up to 12,000 parts, 10,000 of which are moving.
  • It can weigh up to 1,200 pounds or more.



Upright Piano

  • They were originally called "cottage pianos."
  • They are called "upright" pianos because their strings and the soundboard are positioned vertically, perpendicular to the floor.
  • The combined tension of all the strings on a piano is about 20 tons, equivalent to a fully loaded bus.



Keyboard

  • Unlike acoustic pianos with hammers and strings, electric pianos use sensors, amplifiers, and speakers to produce sound electronically.
  • Historically, white keys were covered in ivory, but because of endangerment, most modern keyboards use plastic.
  • A well-maintained digital piano can last between 10 to 20 years.
